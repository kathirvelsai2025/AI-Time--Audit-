# Verification and Responsible-AI Report

## Status
This package is a reproducible project starter with illustrative data. No empirical study has been performed by generating these files.

## Checks completed
- Arithmetic: minutes saved = manual time − AI-assisted time.
- Percentage: time saved percentage = minutes saved / manual time × 100.
- Quality difference: AI quality − manual quality.
- Dataset consistency: six task rows are present in the time and quality exports.
- Chart labels identify time units and quality scale.
- The presentation and report label the figures as illustrative/demo data.

## Items requiring user verification
- Replace all illustrative times and quality ratings with actual observations.
- Confirm task briefs are equivalent between manual and AI-assisted runs.
- Include prompting, review, fact-checking, and rework in AI-assisted timing.
- Score quality with a consistent rubric, ideally by a reviewer unaware of the method.
- Independently verify factual claims and sources in AI outputs.
- Confirm that the chosen AI tools and free-tier limits match what was actually used.
- Keep private, confidential, or identifying information out of prompts unless approved.

## Quality rubric (1–5)
1 = unusable / major errors; 2 = weak / substantial correction needed; 3 = adequate / noticeable gaps; 4 = good / minor corrections; 5 = excellent / accurate and ready to use.

## Risk register
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| AI hallucination | Verify claims against reliable primary sources |
| Hidden correction time | Include review and rework in measured time |
| Unequal task difficulty | Use matched briefs and repeat runs |
| Reviewer bias | Blind the reviewer to production method |
| Privacy exposure | Use synthetic or approved non-sensitive inputs |
| Overclaiming results | Clearly label sample data and report limitations |

## Final verification statement
The calculations in the included example dataset are mechanically reproducible. The values themselves are placeholders for demonstration, not verified observations about real AI productivity.
