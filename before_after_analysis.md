# Before/After Analysis

## Executive summary
This demo compares six common student/office tasks completed manually and with AI assistance. The dataset is illustrative and must not be reported as measured evidence.

| Measure | Illustrative result |
|---|---:|
| Total manual time | 178 minutes |
| Total AI-assisted time | 71 minutes |
| Total time difference | 107 minutes |
| Time saved | 60.1% |
| Average manual quality | 3.83/5 |
| Average AI-assisted quality | 3.83/5 |
| Average quality difference | +0.00 points |

## Interpretation
In this example, AI assistance reduces the combined task time from 178 to 71 minutes. The largest absolute time saving is for the presentation outline (25 minutes), while research comparison shows a lower quality score with AI assistance. This illustrates why speed alone is not enough: source checking and human review may be necessary.

## Where AI may make work worse
- It can produce plausible but unsupported facts or citations.
- It may misunderstand local context, course requirements, or the intended audience.
- The first answer may require substantial correction, reducing or eliminating time savings.
- Spreadsheet formulas may fail on edge cases unless tested.
- Summaries can omit nuance or overstate conclusions.
- Sensitive personal or institutional information should not be pasted into tools without permission.

## Limitations
- All numbers are illustrative, not observed.
- One example per task is not enough to generalise.
- Task difficulty, user familiarity, prompt quality, and model/tool versions affect results.
- Quality ratings are not independent real-world ratings in this demo.
- The comparison should include prompting, verification, and rework in AI time.

## Recommended real-study method
1. Run each task manually and with AI under comparable conditions.
2. Record start/end time and rework time separately.
3. Use the same rubric for both outputs.
4. Have a blind reviewer score outputs where possible.
5. Repeat each task at least three times if time permits.
6. Report the median time and spread, not just one run.
7. Preserve prompts, outputs, tool/version, and verification notes for auditability.

## Conclusion
The correct conclusion from this pack is methodological: AI's time savings should be measured task-by-task and weighed against quality and verification costs. Replace the demo data with measured observations before making claims about actual productivity.
